Why CLTBeat Exists

Charlotte is experiencing one of the biggest transformation cycles in the Southeast.

New infrastructure, rezonings, corporate relocations, and housing demand are reshaping the region in real time.

CLTBeat exists to help people understand what is changing and why it matters.
